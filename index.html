03 / A TOOL, WITH LIMITS

Clear about what
the math can’t know.

The workbook is a planning aid. Its totals rely on numbers you enter and may not match the final contract if charges or payment schedules change. It does not provide legal, tax, financial, lending, valuation or mechanical advice.

State fees and consumer rights vary. For a used vehicle, consider checking its history and asking an independent mechanic to inspect it. Read the lender’s disclosures and signed agreement before you commit.

05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Useful answers,
up front.

No hidden service plan. No data account. No promise that a spreadsheet can make the decision for you.

What exactly would I receive?

One downloadable .xlsx workbook with four tabs: Compare Quotes, Compare Financing, Before You Sign and Start Here. It has labeled fictional examples and blank rows for your own offers.

What software do I need?

The file uses the standard .xlsx format and is designed for Microsoft Excel. Other spreadsheet applications have not been tested, so their layout or formulas may differ.

Does it upload or save my information online?

The workbook has no account, network connection or upload feature. Your entries stay in the spreadsheet file you open and wherever you choose to save that file.

Is checkout automatic?

Checkout is not set up yet. If the product is approved for sale, a digital marketplace can collect payment and deliver the file automatically. The account owner still has to complete seller verification and handle business records and any customer issues.

Will this business run itself forever?

No honest plan can guarantee that. Automated file delivery can remove order-by-order work, but it cannot guarantee sales or eliminate account setup, listing fees, customer support, tax responsibilities or occasional upkeep.
