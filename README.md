# LocalSignal

LocalSignal is in product research. The former agency concept required ongoing client work, and the first digital product candidate (an automotive quote-comparison workbook) did not meet the owner's standard for a broadly useful, compelling product. It has been removed from the public site and is not the current business offer.

## Current public site

The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages and deployed on pushes to `main` by `.github/workflows/pages.yml`. It currently shows a research-stage notice, with no product, customer-data collection, registration, or checkout. GitHub Pages serves static files and does not supply server-side account services. A real registration system will need a managed authentication and data backend, user-specific access controls, privacy and account-deletion flows, and an operating budget. Do not add a pretend signup form before those pieces are ready.

## Product standard

Before choosing a product, validate that it addresses a frequent or consequential consumer problem, gives customers a clear reason to pay compared with free alternatives, can deliver its core value automatically without employees, and can operate with sensible privacy, reliability, cost, and support boundaries. No product is guaranteed to make a profit or run forever without owner oversight.

Healthcare affordability is one area for further investigation, not a decision. Recent KFF polling reports that about one in five U.S. adults had problems paying medical bills in the past year. CMS already offers free medical-bill help, while paid advocacy services also exist. Any product here would need a useful, trustworthy distinction and a safe data-handling model before it could be considered.
